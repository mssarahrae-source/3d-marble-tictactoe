Welcome to 3D Marble Tic-Tac-Toe!

A 3D twist on the classic game. You play on a multi-level shelf with positions stacked vertically.

Get your marbles in a row horizontally, vertically, diagonally, or through multiple levels!

🏭

Two Board Modes

  • 3x3x3 Classic — 3 levels, 27 cells, get 3 in a row (49 winning lines)
  • 4x4x4 Qubic — 4 levels, 64 cells, get 4 in a row (76 winning lines). Far more strategic!

Select your mode from the menu before starting.

👊

Placing Your Marble

Hover over the board to see a ghost marble preview showing where it will land.

Click (or tap on mobile) to place your marble. Players alternate turns.

🌐

Rotating the Board

  • Mouse: click + drag to rotate • scroll to zoom
  • Touch: 1 finger drag to rotate • 2 finger pinch to zoom • 2 finger drag to pan

Rotate often — winning lines through multiple levels are easy to miss!

🏆

Winning

Get your marbles in a straight line — rows, columns, or diagonals on the same level, or lines that pass through multiple levels.

When you win, the board celebrates with fireworks! Click or press any key to continue.

🤖

Playing Against the AI

Select Human vs AI from the menu. Three difficulty levels:

  • Easy — makes occasional mistakes, great for beginners
  • Medium — solid strategic play, beatable
  • Hard — thinks several moves ahead, very tough!
💡

Tips & Tricks

  • The center positions control the most winning lines
  • Watch for threats passing through multiple levels
  • Use Undo to take back a move during play
  • Rotate the board regularly to spot hidden opportunities
